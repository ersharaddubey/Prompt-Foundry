Delete Your Prompt Foundry Account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Prompt Foundry app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us at promptfoundry@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete Account".

What gets deleted

Your profile, saved prompts, and account data are permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.

Some data may be retained where required by law.
